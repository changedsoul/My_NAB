My NAB - a private budgeting app that runs on your own computer
================================================================

My NAB has TWO programs in this folder. Here is what each one does:

  My NAB Server   Starts My NAB in the background. It does not open anything on its own.
                  A small icon appears near the clock to show it is running (click the
                  little ^ arrow if you don't see it). It KEEPS RUNNING when you close your
                  browser, until you right-click the icon and choose "Quit My NAB Server"
                  (or you log off or shut down).

  Open My NAB     Opens your web browser on My NAB. If the server isn't running yet, it
                  offers to start it for you.

TO GET STARTED
  1. Double-click "My NAB Server". (Nothing opens; look for the icon near the clock.)
  2. Double-click "Open My NAB". Your browser shows My NAB.
  3. The first screen lists your budgets. A ready-made "Demo Budget" (made-up data) is
     already there; its password is shown next to it:  demo1234
     Click Open to look around - change anything you like, it's only the demo.
  4. To start your own budget, click "+ New Budget", then "Set Up" and choose a password.
  5. To go back to the list of budgets at any time, click "Close Budget" at the bottom of
     the menu on the left.

TO STOP IT
  Right-click the My NAB Server icon near the clock and choose "Quit My NAB Server".
  Closing the browser does not stop it.

YOUR DATA
  All your budgets are saved in the "data" folder right next to these programs. Back it up
  by copying that folder. To update My NAB later, replace the two .exe files with the new
  ones and keep your "data" folder. You can delete the Demo Budget (trash-can icon on the
  budget list) whenever you like.

IF WINDOWS SAYS "WINDOWS PROTECTED YOUR PC"
  That message appears for any program that isn't from a big publisher. Click
  "More info", then "Run anyway". (If you're unsure, only run it if you got it from
  the official download page.)

NOTES
  * Everything stays on your computer. Nothing is sent anywhere, and it can't be reached
    from other computers.
  * Do not run these from inside the zip file. Right-click the zip, choose
    "Extract All...", then open the extracted folder.
  * If something goes wrong, look for a file called "My NAB.log" next to the programs.
